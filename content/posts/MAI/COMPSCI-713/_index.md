**Classical AI, systematically**

Symbolic logic and knowledge representation, expert systems such as MYCIN, decision trees and ensembles, soft computing (fuzzy logic, Bayesian reasoning), Logic Neural Networks, and neuroevolution with NEAT, plus case studies such as AlphaFold.

These overviews focus on:
- the **mathematical intuition** behind classical AI methods
- connections between **biological systems and computational models**
- understanding *why* algorithms work, not just how to apply them
